Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Who we are

CR8W is a community platform operated by Create Well. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you get it deleted. Questions or deletion requests go to mb@tablante.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us directly: first name and email address when you join the waitlist or create an account.
  • Account identity from Google when you sign in: your email address, name, and profile image.
  • Calendar data you authorize: events, titles, times, and attendee information needed to show and manage your CR8W schedule.
  • Email sending activity you initiate: the recipients and contents of messages the app sends on your instruction.
  • Basic technical logs: IP address, browser type, and timestamps, used for security and debugging.

Google user data and scopes

If you connect your Google account, CR8W requests only the permissions it needs for features you can see:

  • calendar.events to create, update, and remove CR8W events on your calendar when you ask it to.
  • calendar.readonly to display your existing availability so scheduling does not double book you.
  • gmail.send to send confirmations, invitations, and follow ups that you explicitly initiate or approve.

We do not read your inbox for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the feature you triggered.

How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • To show your events, workshops, and RSVPs inside CR8W.
  • To send transactional email you request, such as an RSVP confirmation.
  • To fix bugs and prevent abuse.

Limited use of Google user data

CR8W use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to provide or improve user facing features that are prominent in the app.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No humans read your Google user data except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, to comply with law, or on aggregated and anonymized data for internal operations.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Ever.

Where your data lives

Account records are stored in Supabase. The site is hosted on Vercel. Some operational content is managed in Notion. Google OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and are never exposed to the browser or to other users.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account data while your account is active.
  • You can revoke CR8W access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking deletes the stored tokens.
  • Email mb@tablante.com to delete your account and associated data. We complete deletion within 30 days.
  • Technical logs are retained for up to 90 days.

Service providers

We share only what is necessary with: Google, for authentication and the Calendar and Gmail APIs. Vercel, for hosting. Supabase, for the database. Notion, for internal operations content.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted. Write to mb@tablante.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

CR8W is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and notify account holders by email.
